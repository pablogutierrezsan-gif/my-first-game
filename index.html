Alien Tic-Tac-Toe

Goal: get three X in a row before the aliens get three O.

  1. Pick an empty square. A battle starts for that square.
  2. The alien attacks. Choose the response that counters it.
  3. Win the battle and you place X. Lose it and the alien places O.
  4. Three X in a line wins. Three O loses. A full board with no line is a draw.
